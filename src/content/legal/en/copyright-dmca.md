## 4. How to file an infringement notice (takedown)

For the notice to be valid (17 U.S.C. §512(c)(3) or equivalent), it must include **all** of these elements:

1. A physical or electronic signature of the copyright owner or of a person authorized to act on their behalf.
2. Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to be infringed (or a list of works if there are several).
3. Identification of the infringing material to be removed and **information reasonably sufficient** to locate it within the Platform (URL, club, user, screen, etc.).
4. Contact information of the complainant: name, address, telephone, and email.
5. A statement of **good faith** that use of the material is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
6. A statement, **under penalty of perjury**, that the information is accurate and that the complainant is authorized to act on behalf of the copyright owner.

**We will process complete notices within a reasonable period** (usually 2–5 business days), removing or disabling the allegedly infringing material and informing the affected User.
