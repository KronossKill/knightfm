# Cookie Policy — Knight FM

> **Notice:** legal template subject to attorney review. Knight FM uses **only its own technical storage** (essential cookies + `localStorage`/`sessionStorage`) and an **optional first-party analytics** subject to consent. There is no third-party advertising tracking.

**Last updated:** [FECHA] · **Version:** 1.0

## 1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small files the site stores on your device. We also use equivalent technologies: the browser's `localStorage` and `sessionStorage` (local storage). In this policy we call all of them "cookies".

## 2. Cookies and storage we use

| Name / key | Type | Duration | Purpose | Third parties |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `knightfm.auth` (sessionStorage) | technical (essential) | until the tab closes | keep you signed in securely (access/refresh tokens with our own rotation) | No |
| `knightfm.consent` (localStorage) | technical (essential) | persistent | remember your decision about analytics (cookie banner) | No |
| `knightfm.theme` (localStorage) | preferences | persistent | remember your visual theme | No |
| `knightfm.locale` (localStorage) | preferences | persistent | remember your language (ES/EN/FR/PT) | No |
| captcha widget (Turnstile or other) | technical | during the form | prevent bots and abuse on registration/login/recovery | Cloudflare or another provider, if enabled |
| first-party analytics events (via `fetch` to the API) | first-party analytics | stored in the platform's database | measure interface usage (sections viewed, clicks) in order to improve it | No |

## 3. First-party analytics and consent

3.1. Knight FM's analytics is **first-party and anonymous**: events are pseudonymized and emails and identifying data are removed from the content before storage. We do not use Google Analytics or third-party advertising tools.

3.2. **Analytics remains disabled until you press "Accept"** in the cookie banner. If you press "Reject", no analytics event is sent; the game works exactly the same without it.

3.3. You can change your decision at any time from the **"Cookies"** link in the footer, which reopens the banner.

## 4. We do not use third-party advertising cookies

There are no profiling cookies, behavioral advertising, or social media pixels. If a third party is incorporated in the future (e.g., product analytics), this policy will be updated and prior consent requested, with rejection as easy as acceptance.

## 5. How to manage them

- From the platform's **cookie banner** (accept / reject / reopen from the footer).
- From your browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge: "Privacy and security" section) you can delete or block cookies and local storage.
- **Important:** if you block the technical storage (`knightfm.auth`), the session will not work and you will not be able to sign in.

## 6. Legal basis

- **Technical and essential:** legitimate interest / performance of the contract (exemption under art. 5.3 LSSI and the provision of art. 22.2 LSSI).
- **First-party analytics:** consent via the banner (art. 6.1.a RGPD).
